About Me

  • Education: BS Computer Engineering, Dr. Yanga's Colleges Inc.
  • Experience:~3 years as a cellphone technician
  • Currently learning: HTML, CSS, and web development

My Journey

The Hardware Phase

My journey actually began with fixing cellphones—those tiny, frustrating, wonderful little devices—and somewhere in the middle of troubleshooting hardware, squinting at circuit boards, and figuring out why a phone that looked perfectly fine refused to turn on, I learned patience I didn't know I had, developed a kind of logical thinking that follows every clue from symptom to source, and came to understand how all these tiny, seemingly insignificant components work together in ways you'd never expect to form something bigger than themselves—a whole system, a whole device, a whole working phone that someone depends on.

Discovering Code

While studying Computer Engineering, I had this moment of realization that software is every bit as fascinating as hardware—maybe even more, in some ways—and so I started teaching myself Python, fumbling through the basics, celebrating small wins when something finally ran without errors, and eventually branching out into HTML and CSS so I could actually bring my ideas to life on the web, turning abstract thoughts into something people could see, click, and interact with, which honestly felt a little like magic after years of thinking in circuits and components.
